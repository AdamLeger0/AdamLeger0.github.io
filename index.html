About & Focus

I am a political scientist currently pursuing a Master of Liberal Arts in Political Science at Winthrop University. My academic work sits at the intersection of political theory, international institutions, and governance evolution. I construct typologies and analytical frameworks to examine how authority is delegated within international and future structural orders.

Beyond theoretical research, I serve as a Graduate Assistant within Winthrop University's School of Graduate, Continuing, and Online Education, as well as an Assistant Director with the Southern Regional Model United Nations (SRMUN). My broader focus encompasses civic engagement, political communication, education policy, and youth leadership.
